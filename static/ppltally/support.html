PPL Tally: Support

A simple push, pull, legs workout log for iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: ppltally@amrthabit.com. Every email gets read.

Quick answers

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server.

How do I back it up? Use export to save your log as a CSV file, and keep your phone's own backups on.

Kilograms? Tap any weight to show it in kg.

Two gyms? Tap the gym name at the top of the start screen and add one. Each gym is tracked on its own.

More help is in the app: tap the i at the top of the start screen.
