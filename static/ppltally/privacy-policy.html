PPL Tally: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

PPL Tally is a workout log. Everything you record in it stays on your iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. PPL Tally has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your workouts, your bodyweight or anything else to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives your data.

What stays on your phone

The sets, exercises, weights, gyms and bodyweight you enter are stored only on your device. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under Apple's terms, not ours.

Exporting

You can export your log as a CSV file. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
